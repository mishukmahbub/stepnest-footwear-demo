Delivery and payments

A summary of the fictional New York City store policies used by the FAQ assistant.

Delivery

$5.95 within New York City; $8.95 to other addresses in the contiguous United States. Standard shipping is free on merchandise subtotals of $75.00 or more before shipping and tax. Delivery estimates start after dispatch.

Payment

Major credit/debit cards through secure checkout, and PayPal if shown at checkout. Prices are before applicable sales tax. Never share card details or verification codes in chat.

Size guide

Measure your foot from heel to the longest toe in centimetres and compare it with the approximate US men’s size chart in the guide. Fit varies by product; the assistant cannot guarantee a perfect fit.

Returns and exchanges

For this practice project, return/exchange requests must be raised within 30 calendar days. Shoes must be unworn and unused with the original box, tags, and packaging. Wrong or defective items must be reported within 48 hours with photos. The store reviews every request; the bot cannot approve it.

Demo notice: StepNest Footwear is fictional. All prices, products and policies on this page are for learning and portfolio practice, not real commercial offers.

Have a question?
